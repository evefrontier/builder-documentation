# Smart Infrastructure

### Smart Infrastructure

**EVE Frontier** is designed as an open environment - a sandbox with a heightened level of player agency. With a complete unlock of third-party development, the game world will evolve with the community - not just through their in-game actions, but by allowing it to be edited server-side by players in real-time. Through enhanced base-building (**Smart Assemblies**), players can code functionality into infrastructure placed in space such as turrets, trading posts, storage facilities, and more. Develop your own ideas or address the needs of others: from mission systems to private economies, **Smart Assemblies** unlock third-party development with a powerful toolkit for anyone to build beyond a virtual world.

> **Note:** **Constructing infrastructure and deploying it on the Frontier does not require it to be programmed.**

\*\*Smart\*\* refers to any object that can connect outside the game via a programmable layer, linking the virtual world of \*\*EVE Frontier\*\* with the material world through blockchain.

### Smart Objects

A core concept in **EVE Frontier** are **Smart Objects** which act as the foundational layer of programmable entities, providing a unified interface that translates the in-game ontology into an on-chain environment.

The **Smart Objects** in **EVE Frontier** are the base set of tools that empower builders to reshape and redefine the game’s universe. These programmable, blockchain-enabled entities act as versatile building blocks, allowing creators to construct and customize the in-game environment and interact with the real world. Through this system, **EVE Frontier** provides an unprecedented level of player-driven world-building, with **Smart Objects** serving as the core elements that will drive the reconstruction and expansion of the game’s universe over time.

\*\*Digital physics\*\* refers to digital objects that are constrained by the game world rules as if they were physical things in the real world. \_For example, interactions between physical objects require those objects to be in a single location in space (or very near proximity). This is also true of game objects that are bound by the game world's digital physics. Even if they are represented on-chain, they are still bound to a specific location in the game world.\_

### Current infrastructure

Currently, **Smart Objects** in **EVE Frontier** consist of two key categories:

* **Smart Assemblies**, which interact directly with the game’s mechanics and are bound by the game world digital physics, and
* **Smart Characters**, which represent on-chain player/character identities and are not constrained by these physical rules.

**Smart Assemblies**—objects in space—follow game enforced laws of physics similar to the laws that apply to real world physical entities, while **Smart Characters** manage your in-game identity and assets/interactions on the blockchain. Looking forward, the plan is to expand the arsenal of **Smart Objects**, adding more programmable and interactive tools for builders to influence and reshape the **EVE Frontier** universe.
